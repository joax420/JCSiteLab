// FAQ

COMMON
QUESTIONS

How long does it take?

A landing page can be ready in around a week when content is prepared. Full websites usually depend on page count, content, and feedback speed.

Do you do revisions?

Yes. Packages include revision rounds so the site can be refined before launch without dragging the project out forever.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, goals, services, images or brand assets if available, example sites you like, and clear feedback at review points.

Can you help with copy?

Yes. I can help structure and tighten the copy so each section is clear, useful, and focused on the action you want visitors to take.
